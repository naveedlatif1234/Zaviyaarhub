We aim to deliver across Pakistan, subject to courier availability.

Delivery charges are confirmed before dispatch.

Please provide your complete address and active phone number.

Return & Exchange Policy

Report damaged, defective or incorrect products within 48 hours of delivery.

Photos or videos may be required.

Products must be unused and in original packaging where applicable.

Used cosmetics and hygiene-sensitive items are not eligible for return unless defective or incorrectly delivered.

Change-of-mind returns are not accepted unless agreed.

Approved returns, exchanges or refunds will be reviewed and arranged accordingly.
